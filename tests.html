About Our Laboratory Tests

Laboratory tests help healthcare professionals understand a patient's health condition. A sample such as blood, urine or other body fluids is collected and analyzed to identify important health indicators.

Depending on the test, results may help in early detection, diagnosis, monitoring of treatment and routine health screening. The interpretation of every report should be done with the advice of a qualified healthcare professional.

Major Categories of Tests

  • Hematology: Tests related to blood cells and blood disorders.
  • Biochemistry: Measures substances such as glucose, enzymes and proteins.
  • Urinalysis: Examines urine for physical and chemical indicators.
  • Hormone Testing: Helps evaluate hormone levels in the body.
  • Microbiology: Helps identify microorganisms causing infections.

Important Test Information

Complete Blood Count (CBC)
Provides information about red blood cells, white blood cells and platelets.

Urine Routine Examination
Examines urine for physical, chemical and microscopic characteristics.

HbA1c
Provides an estimate of average blood glucose levels over the previous few months.

Thyroid Profile
Measures thyroid-related hormones to help evaluate thyroid function.

Remember: Test requirements and reference ranges may vary. Always follow the instructions given by your doctor or laboratory.
