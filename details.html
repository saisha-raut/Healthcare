Test Overview

Test Name Urine Routine Examination
Sample Required Fresh Midstream Urine Sample
Purpose Evaluation of physical, chemical and microscopic properties of urine
Report Time Within 24 Hours
Fasting Required Generally Not Required

Preparation Instructions

  • Use a clean and sterile sample container.
  • Collect a fresh midstream urine sample.
  • Submit the sample to the laboratory as soon as possible.
  • Follow additional instructions given by your doctor.

Parameters Commonly Checked

Colour, appearance, pH, glucose, protein, ketones, blood and other microscopic findings may be evaluated.

Note: Test results must be interpreted by a qualified healthcare professional.
