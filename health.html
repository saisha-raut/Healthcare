Health Awareness

Understanding common health conditions and recognising possible warning signs can encourage people to seek appropriate medical care.

Diabetes

Diabetes is a condition in which the body cannot properly regulate blood glucose levels.

Common Symptoms

  • Increased thirst
  • Frequent urination
  • Fatigue
  • Blurred vision
  • Unexplained weight loss

Screening and Diagnosis

Blood glucose measurements such as fasting plasma glucose and HbA1c can be used as part of diabetes assessment.

Prevention and Healthy Habits

  • Regular physical activity
  • Healthy diet
  • Maintaining a healthy body weight
  • Avoiding tobacco
  • Regular health checkups

Heart Health

Regular health screening, physical activity and a healthy lifestyle can support cardiovascular health.

Possible Warning Signs

  • Chest discomfort
  • Breathing difficulty
  • Unusual fatigue
  • Dizziness

Serious or sudden symptoms require immediate professional medical attention.

Kidney Health

Kidney health can be assessed through appropriate medical evaluation and laboratory investigations.

  • Regular health checkups
  • Blood pressure monitoring
  • Appropriate blood tests
  • Urine testing when advised
